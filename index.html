YOUR INVESTIGATION

Your Lab Route

Each stage increases in difficulty. You will move from molecular construction to analysis, error diagnosis, and evidence-based evaluation.

01

Construct a Nucleotide

Identify and assemble the correct molecular components of a DNA nucleotide.

02

Analyze the Bases

Investigate nitrogenous-base structures and determine how the four DNA bases can be classified.

03

Construct a DNA Strand

Build a polynucleotide strand and analyze the sugar-phosphate backbone.

04

Construct the Complement

Build the complementary strand and analyze base pairing and hydrogen bonding.

05

Diagnose the DNA

Locate and repair structural errors in a double-stranded DNA model.

06

Investigate DNA Structure

Evaluate competing DNA models using multiple pieces of molecular evidence.

FINAL CHALLENGE

Construct and defend a complete DNA molecule.

At the end of the investigation, you will combine everything you have learned to build a biologically valid DNA molecule, analyze its structure, and reveal a fully labelled double helix.
