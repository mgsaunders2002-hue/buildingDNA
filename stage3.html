STAGE 03

Construct a DNA Strand

A DNA strand is more than a sequence of letters. Use your knowledge of nucleotide structure to construct a strand from molecular components, identify how neighbouring nucleotides are connected, and evaluate different models of DNA structure.

PART A — MOLECULAR CONSTRUCTION

Build the required DNA strand.

Select molecular components from the tray and use them to construct the six nucleotides in the required DNA strand. Build each nucleotide vertically, working down one numbered column at a time. Each numbered column represents one complete nucleotide. Complete nucleotide 1 before moving across to nucleotide 2, and continue until all six nucleotides are built.

You may drag a component into the model or click a component and then click its destination. Do not build horizontally across the rows.

How to build your strand
